Mission
TO BE THE PLACE TO BEGIN FOR ALL BAY AREA CHILDREN, YOUTH AND FAMILIES WHO NEED MENTAL HEALTH, SOCIAL SERVICES AND ACADEMIC SUPPORT. WE WORK TO MAKE THIS VISION A REALITY IN THE SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA BY HELPING VULNERABLE CHILDREN AND FAMILIES OVERCOME SOME OF LIFE'S TOUGHEST CHALLENGES - ABUSE, NEGLECT, MENTAL ILLNESS, AND FAMILY CRISIS. OUR MISSION IS TO STRENGTHEN CHILDREN, YOUTH, FAMILIES, AND THEIR COMMUNITIES THROUGH SERVICE, TRAINING, ADVOCACY, AND RESEARCH.
