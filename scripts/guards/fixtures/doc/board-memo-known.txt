Board Memo: Year-End Giving Campaign

To: Development Committee, Harbor Lights Youth Alliance. From: Director of Development. Date: November 3, 2025.

Purpose

In Northshore County, staff at the Young Adult Housing Bridge report donors who say they do not know what their gift achieved. The board has asked leadership to treat this as a priority because it shapes how families first experience the organization and how faith communities judge whether their support matters.

Our working goal for this period is to build a monthly-giving circle of 400 members. We will measure progress quarterly and report it to the finance committee, with a plain summary shared with faith communities.

Interviews with 27 first-time online donors found that the most common reason for giving was a personal connection to a young person served by the Mentor Match, while the most common reason for lapsing was not hearing back after the first gift.

The Young Adult Housing Bridge served 1350 young people in fiscal year 2023, of whom 78 percent completed the planned course of support. Families named a donor database that does not record why people first gave as the main barrier.

What happened last year

Fundraising strategy for what happened last year rests on three moves: deepen relationships with faith communities, tell specific stories from the Young Adult Housing Bridge, and give every donor a way to see results within ninety days of their gift.

Risks to this plan include reliance on Eastvale County contract renewals, the cost of recruiting licensed clinicians, and the time development staff can give to first-time online donors while also running two annual events.

In Bayview County, staff at the Mentor Match report waitlists of six to ten weeks for counseling intake. The board has asked leadership to treat this as a priority because it shapes how families first experience the organization and how planned-giving supporters judge whether their support matters.

Our working goal for this period is to diversify revenue so no single county contract exceeds 30 percent. We will measure progress quarterly and report it to the finance committee, with a plain summary shared with family foundations.
