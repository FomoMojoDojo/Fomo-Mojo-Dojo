1. Company Overview

Company Name: FomoMojoDojo

Product: MojoMap™

Category: Strategic Decision System

Description:

FomoMojoDojo builds Strategic Decision Systems that help organizations stop guessing and start making better decisions. Its core product, MojoMap™, is a living, evidence-driven map that connects customer truth, strategic choices, and execution into one shared system. The company combines consulting-led engagements with a product vision to create a repeatable system that improves decision quality, aligns teams, and drives momentum.

⸻

2. Strategic Objective (Map Outcome)

Build a category-defining Strategic Decision System (MojoMap™) and achieve consistent high-value engagements ($50K–$100K+) while progressing toward a scalable product.

⸻

3. Core Job-to-be-Done (ODI / JTBD)

When leading a team or organization, help me decide what to focus on and what to do next so that we achieve our desired outcomes with confidence and speed.
