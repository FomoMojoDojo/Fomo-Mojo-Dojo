# Product Context

## Product

MojoMap is a strategy operating system used in consulting engagements.

It helps teams answer:

1. What is our current likelihood of initiative success?
2. What is the clearest next move to improve that likelihood?

## Core idea

The score is a diagnostic, not the deliverable.

The real output is a traceable path from low-confidence assumptions to evidence-backed decisions and execution.

## Three phases

1. Diagnose
- Gather public, internal, and market evidence.
- Identify assumptions and unknowns.

2. Focus
- Prioritize one outcome and the best route(s).
- Make explicit choices and tradeoffs.

3. Flow
- Implement, measure, and monitor.
- Validate assumptions and adjust scoring from real evidence.

## Key generated artifacts

- Strategy Cascade
- Positioning Canvas
- ODI/JTBD Job Maps and outcomes
- Opportunities
- Routes (Fix / Improve / Create)
- Current reality and forward signal

## Framework alignment required

- JTBD / ODI outcomes and job maps
- Positioning rigor (competitive alternatives, category, differentiated value)
- Strategy cascade coherence (where to play/how to win/capabilities/systems/assumptions)
- GTM execution realism
